## Scene checks

Keep frame geometry, occupied alpha pixels and physics geometry distinct. On a grounded pose, compute the last occupied alpha row from the actual loaded image and compare its world-space edge with the actual body bottom. This mathematical alignment does not prove the body matches Kipu's core silhouette or that humans accept the art.

Use muted scenery, distinct decorative roof colors, bright continuous walkable top edges and darker undersides. These are project decisions; no source mandates this exact palette or sprite size. Preserve route coordinates, parallax and mechanics during visual-only work.

Review the same pose on ground, facing left, after restart and on a platform. Include real-input regression for controls, focus clearing, projectile pooling, camera and lifecycle. Any future new art request includes intended role, composition, transparency, adaptation target, exclusions and acceptance criteria.
